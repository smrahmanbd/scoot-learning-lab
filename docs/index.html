Learn SCOOT by running it

SCOOT counts traffic far upstream, predicts the queue before it forms, and nudges splits, offsets and cycle times a few seconds at a time. This lab teaches it in three levels, with simulators you can drive in the browser and a SCOOT-style controller for Eclipse SUMO.

What is in the lab

Browser simulators

Fifteen interactive models: a live junction with SCOOT's model queue, an LPU calculator, platoon dispersion, the queue model, a validation exercise, the three optimisers, gating, bus priority and research toys, including a split agent you train in the browser.

SUMO controller

A Python/TraCI SCOOT-style controller with link models and all three optimisers, a four-junction region, fixed and actuated baselines, and 11 scripts that reproduce every figure on this site.
